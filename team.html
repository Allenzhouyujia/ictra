Who presents what

Each member owns one branch of the evidence on this site, presents it in 30 seconds, and takes the questions on it. Links go to the exhibit each person is responsible for.

H

Helena

EEG branch · Model A · montage · website

What exists in this branch

  • Model A results: 0.98 of convulsive seizures, 0.37 overall; the EEG-only reference every other model is compared with.
  • The montage finding: right-sided montage looked worse (0.17 vs 0.51) until the three short-seizure patients were separated — then 0.45.
  • Feature design: band powers 1–25 Hz, line length, Δ against the 5-minute median.

30-second part

"Two electrodes behind the ear see rhythmic discharges within seconds. Alone they catch nearly every convulsive seizure but only a third of focal ones — that number is the floor the other signals have to lift."

Questions to own

Why line length? Why 1–25 Hz? What does the behind-the-ear EEG miss, and why did the right montage look worse?

v04 montage-aware features; CHB-MIT and Siena loaders through the T7–P7 / T8–P8 proxy; the website replay demo.

C

Carina

ECG branch · Model B · the timing study

What exists in this branch

  • Exp R, Exhibit 1: the heart reacts 4 s after the brain in convulsive seizures, together with it in focal ones, and in only 58 % of non-motor seizures (median rise 22 bpm).
  • Exp P: 538 seizures vs 5,350 controls — no drift in the two minutes before onset.
  • Data audit: the flat / saturated ECG hours (8 h at a constant value in one file) that are now masked for every model.
  • Model B vs A: +0.043 F1, not robust across model families — ECG alone adds little; its value appears inside Model C.

30-second part

"We measured when the heart moves relative to the brain in 695 seizures. In convulsive seizures it follows 4 seconds later; in focal ones it moves at the same time. So heart rate adds coverage, not speed — and in the two minutes before a seizure there is nothing to see."

Questions to own

Why is H3 rewritten? What is the control in Exp P? Why does rule_hr beat the models on seizures under 15 s?

Next

v04 relationship features (HR rise within 20 s of an EEG jump); Billeci and szdb adapters.

T

Tiffany

EMG + motion branch · what Model C adds

What exists in this branch

  • Model C vs A: +11 points of sensitivity at 1 FA/day, F1 +0.064 [+0.023, +0.095], P(improve) = 0.998, better in all 5 folds — the gain is EMG and movement, not ECG.
  • Exp R: muscle and movement arrive 8–12 s after the brain in convulsive seizures and peak at z ≈ 30–40; they also react in 59 % of "non-motor" seizures, late — movement after onset, not a motor sign.
  • The dataset decision: three Kaggle "EMG seizure" sets were duplicates or synthetic; SeizeIT2 deltoid EMG is the only open training source.

30-second part

"Muscle and movement are the most specific signals we have: in convulsive seizures they go off the scale. Adding them to EEG and heart catches 11 points more seizures at the same false-alarm rate — that is the single biggest gain in the project."

Questions to own

Why the deltoid? Why not a wrist watch? What does the accelerometer see that EMG does not?

Next

The Arbune 2020 study (ictal EMG → post-ictal EEG suppression); the My Seizure Gauge external test.

C

Constance

Fusion, evaluation and honesty

What exists in this branch

  • The three tiers: 96 development patients in 5 folds, 29 locked, external sets — and why the SzCORE challenge's 80–90 % → 2–43 % drop is the thing to avoid.
  • Dead ends and negative results: H3 rewritten, the 70 % threshold rejected, the seed-dependent τ, the datasets not used, the metrics not reported.
  • The accept rule (F1 +0.01, P ≥ 0.90, sensitivity −0.02, FA +0.5) and the version log that every change passes through.
  • The ethics and limits: 40 % of non-motor seizures leave no trace; not a medical device.

30-second part

"Our first version catches 97 % of convulsive seizures at one false alarm a day and fails on short focal ones — and we can show you exactly which patients and why. Every number here comes from a patient we never trained on, under a rule we wrote before we looked."

Questions to own

Why not accuracy? What is a patient bootstrap? What happens if the locked test disagrees with the development set?

v06 late fusion with modality dropout; the pre-registration document; the independent re-computation script.
